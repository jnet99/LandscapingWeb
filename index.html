Q&A

Common questions

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. Service is recurring but month-to-month — cancel or pause anytime with 48 hours' notice before your next visit.

What areas do you service?

We proudly serve Owasso and the surrounding areas, including Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Claremore, Collinsville, and Skiatook. Contact us to confirm your address is in range.

What's included in a standard mow?

Mowing, edging along hard surfaces, and blowing off walks, drives, and porches. Bed trimming is included on select plans.

Do you offer one-time yard cleanups?

Yes! We offer one-time cleanups for overgrown yards, seasonal debris, leaves, weeds, and general yard maintenance.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes — mowers, trimmers, and blowers are all provided by us. You don't need to leave anything out except gate access.

Do you offer recurring lawn maintenance?

Yes. We offer recurring services on a weekly, biweekly, or customized schedule depending on your property's needs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Zelle, Cash App, and PayPal.
